## Popular Ghazals

### Most Famous/Widely Known

1. **Hazaaron Khwaishein Aisi** - Mirza Ghalib
   - One of Ghalib's most famous couplets about unfulfilled desires

2. **Dil Hi To Hai** - Mirza Ghalib
   - Classic ghazal about the heart and its emotions

3. **Mujh Se Pehli Si Mohabbat** - Faiz Ahmad Faiz
   - Romantic ghazal that became iconic

4. **Hum Dekhenge** - Faiz Ahmad Faiz
   - Revolutionary and philosophical poem

5. **Ranjish Hi Sahi** - Ahmad Faraz
   - Beautiful ghazal about love and longing
