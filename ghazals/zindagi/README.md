# Zindagi (Life - Vida)

This directory contains ghazals and shayari centered on the theme of **life** (ज़िंदगी - Zindagi).

## About This Theme

Life and its experiences form a rich theme in Urdu poetry. These ghazals reflect on the journey of life, its joys and sorrows, the passage of time, and the wisdom gained through living.

## Common Themes

- **Zindagi** (ज़िंदगी): Life and living
- **Waqt** (वक़्त): Time and its passage
- **Tajurba** (तजुर्बा): Experience
- **Safar** (सफ़र): Journey of life
- **Umeed** (उम्मीद): Hope
- **Hausla** (हौसला): Courage and determination

## What to Contribute

We welcome ghazals that explore:
- Reflections on the meaning of life
- Lessons learned through experience
- The passage of time
- Balance between joy and sorrow
- Resilience and perseverance
