# Judai (Separation - Separación)

This directory contains ghazals and shayari centered on the theme of **separation** (जुदाई - Judai).

## About This Theme

Separation from the beloved (hijr - हिज्र) is one of the most poignant themes in Urdu poetry. These ghazals express the pain of distance, unfulfilled longing, and the torment of being apart from one's love.

## Ghazals in This Collection

1. **Ye Na Thi Hamari Qismat** - Mirza Ghalib
   - One of the most famous ghazals about unfulfilled destiny and the pain of separation

## Common Themes

- **Hijr** (हिज्र): The state of separation from the beloved
- **Intezaar** (इंतज़ार): Waiting and longing
- **Tanhai** (तन्हाई): Loneliness
- **Yaad** (याद): Memories of the beloved
- **Gham** (ग़म): The sorrow of separation
