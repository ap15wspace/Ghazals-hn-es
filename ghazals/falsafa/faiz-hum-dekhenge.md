# हम देखेंगे (Hum Dekhenge)

**Poet:** Faiz Ahmad Faiz (फ़ैज़ अहमद फ़ैज़)  
**Theme:** Falsafa (Philosophy)  
**Era:** 20th Century (1911-1984)

---

## Original (Hindi/Urdu - Devanagari)

हम देखेंगे  
लाज़िम है कि हम भी देखेंगे  
वो दिन कि जिसका वादा है  
जो लौह-ए-अज़ल में लिखा है

हम देखेंगे

जब ज़ुल्म-ओ-सितम के कोह-ए-गिरां  
रूई की तरह उड़ जाएंगे  
हम महकूमों के पाँव तले  
ये धरती धड़-धड़ धड़केगी  
और अहल-ए-हकम के सर ऊपर  
जब बिजली कड़-कड़ कड़केगी

हम देखेंगे

जब अर्ज़-ए-ख़ुदा के काबे से  
सब बुत उठवाए जाएंगे  
हम अहल-ए-सफ़ा मरदूद-ए-हरम  
मसनद पे बिठाए जाएंगे  
सब ताज उछाले जाएंगे  
सब तख़्त गिराए जाएंगे

हम देखेंगे

बस नाम रहेगा अल्लाह का  
जो ग़ायब भी है हाज़िर भी  
जो मंज़र भी है नाज़िर भी  
उठेगा अन-अल-हक़ का नारा  
जो मैं भी हूँ और तुम भी हो  
और राज करेगी ख़ल्क़-ए-ख़ुदा  
जो मैं भी हूँ और तुम भी हो

हम देखेंगे  
लाज़िम है कि हम भी देखेंगे

## Transliteration (Romanized)

Hum dekhenge  
Laazim hai ki hum bhi dekhenge  
Wo din ki jiska waada hai  
Jo lauh-e-azal mein likha hai

Hum dekhenge

Jab zulm-o-sitam ke koh-e-giraan  
Rui ki tarah ud jaayenge  
Hum mehkumon ke paon tale  
Ye dharti dhad-dhad dhadkegi  
Aur ahl-e-hakam ke sar oopar  
Jab bijli kad-kad kadkegi

Hum dekhenge

Jab arz-e-Khuda ke kaabe se  
Sab but uthwaaye jaayenge  
Hum ahl-e-safa mardood-e-haram  
Masnad pe bithaye jaayenge  
Sab taaj uchhaale jaayenge  
Sab takht giraaye jaayenge

Hum dekhenge

Bas naam rahega Allah ka  
Jo ghaayab bhi hai haazir bhi  
Jo manzar bhi hai naazir bhi  
Uthega an-al-haq ka naara  
Jo main bhi hoon aur tum bhi ho  
Aur raaj karegi khalq-e-Khuda  
Jo main bhi hoon aur tum bhi ho

Hum dekhenge  
Laazim hai ki hum bhi dekhenge

## Spanish Translation (Traducción Española)

Lo veremos  
Es inevitable que también lo veremos  
Ese día que está prometido  
Que está escrito en la tabla eterna

Lo veremos

Cuando las montañas de opresión y tiranía  
Vuelen como algodón  
Bajo los pies de nosotros los oprimidos  
Esta tierra palpitará intensamente  
Y sobre las cabezas de los gobernantes  
Cuando el rayo truene estruendosamente

Lo veremos

Cuando de la Kaaba de Dios  
Todos los ídolos sean removidos  
Nosotros, la gente pura, rechazados del santuario  
Seremos sentados en el trono  
Todas las coronas serán lanzadas al aire  
Todos los tronos serán derribados

Lo veremos

Solo quedará el nombre de Alá  
Quien está ausente y también presente  
Quien es el espectáculo y también el espectador  
Se levantará el grito de "Yo soy la Verdad"  
Que soy yo y también eres tú  
Y gobernará la creación de Dios  
Que soy yo y también eres tú

Lo veremos  
Es inevitable que también lo veremos

## Context / Notes

"Hum Dekhenge" is one of the most powerful revolutionary poems in Urdu literature. Written in 1979 during the military dictatorship of General Zia-ul-Haq in Pakistan, this nazm (poem) became an anthem of resistance and hope for oppressed people.

The poem uses Islamic imagery and Quranic references to convey a message of social justice and equality. The phrase "An-al-Haq" (I am the Truth) refers to the mystic Mansur Al-Hallaj who was executed for declaring this, symbolizing the ultimate sacrifice for truth.

Faiz masterfully combines religious symbolism with revolutionary ideals, suggesting that true faith lies in justice and equality, not in the authority of oppressive rulers. The poem envisions a day when all forms of tyranny will end and ordinary people will prevail.

This poem has been recited at protests and demonstrations across South Asia and continues to inspire movements for democracy and human rights. It was notably sung by Iqbal Bano in 1985 at a concert in Lahore, which became a historic moment of defiance against dictatorship.

---

## About the Poet

**Faiz Ahmad Faiz** (1911-1984) was a Pakistani revolutionary poet, intellectual, and human rights activist. He used poetry as a tool for social change and spent several years in prison for his political beliefs. "Hum Dekhenge" represents the pinnacle of his commitment to justice and his ability to inspire hope even in the darkest times. He remains one of the most celebrated and influential poets in South Asian history.
