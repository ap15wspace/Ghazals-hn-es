# Falsafa (Philosophy - Filosofía)

This directory contains ghazals and shayari centered on **philosophical themes** (फ़लसफ़ा - Falsafa).

## About This Theme

Philosophy in Urdu poetry encompasses reflections on life, death, existence, spirituality, social justice, and the nature of reality. These ghazals move beyond personal emotions to address universal questions and truths.

## Ghazals in This Collection

1. **Hum Dekhenge** - Faiz Ahmad Faiz
   - Revolutionary poem about justice, equality, and hope for the oppressed

## Common Themes

- **Hayaat** (हयात): Life and its meaning
- **Maut** (मौत): Death and mortality
- **Insaniyat** (इंसानियत): Humanity and human values
- **Haq** (हक़): Truth and justice
- **Wujood** (वुजूद): Existence and being
- **Khudi** (ख़ुदी): Self and ego
- **Tasawwuf** (तसव्वुफ़): Mysticism and Sufism

## Philosophical Traditions

Urdu poetry draws from multiple philosophical traditions:
- **Sufism**: Mystical Islam and divine love
- **Persian Philosophy**: Classical wisdom and metaphysics
- **Progressive Thought**: Social justice and equality
- **Existentialism**: Modern reflections on existence
