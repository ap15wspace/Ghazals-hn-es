# Khushi (Happiness - Felicidad)

This directory contains ghazals and shayari centered on the theme of **happiness** (ख़ुशी - Khushi).

## About This Theme

While Urdu poetry is often associated with melancholy, joy and celebration are also important themes. These ghazals express moments of bliss, gratitude, celebration, and the simple pleasures of life.

## Common Themes

- **Khushi** (ख़ुशी): Happiness and joy
- **Masti** (मस्ती): Merriment and revelry
- **Jashn** (जश्न): Celebration
- **Shukrana** (शुक्राना): Gratitude
- **Suroor** (सुरूर): Ecstasy and bliss
- **Umang** (उमंग): Enthusiasm and excitement

## What to Contribute

We welcome ghazals that celebrate:
- Moments of pure joy
- Gratitude for life's blessings
- Festive occasions and celebrations
- The beauty of existence
- Simple pleasures
