# Hasrat (Desire/Longing - Anhelo)

This directory contains ghazals and shayari centered on the theme of **desire and longing** (हसरत - Hasrat).

## About This Theme

Hasrat represents unfulfilled desires, yearning, and aspirations. These ghazals explore the human condition of wanting what seems unattainable, the beauty in longing, and the poetry of unfulfilled dreams.

## Ghazals in This Collection

1. **Hazaaron Khwaishein Aisi** - Mirza Ghalib
   - The iconic ghazal about countless desires that remain unfulfilled

## Common Themes

- **Khwaahish** (ख़्वाहिश): Desire and wish
- **Aarzu** (आरज़ू): Longing and aspiration
- **Tamanna** (तमन्ना): Strong desire or craving
- **Hasrat** (हसरत): Unfulfilled longing
- **Armaan** (अरमान): Cherished wishes

## The Beauty of Unfulfillment

In Urdu poetry, unfulfilled desire is often celebrated because:
- It represents the infinite capacity of the human heart
- The journey of longing is as important as attainment
- Desire keeps the soul alive and striving
- Some things are more beautiful in their pursuit than in possession
