# Dard (Pain/Sorrow - Dolor)

This directory contains ghazals and shayari centered on the theme of **pain and sorrow** (दर्द - Dard).

## About This Theme

Pain and sorrow are fundamental to the human experience and feature prominently in Urdu poetry. These ghazals explore emotional suffering, heartbreak, existential anguish, and the beauty that can emerge from pain.

## Ghazals in This Collection

1. **Gulon Mein Rang Bhare** - Jaun Elia
   - Melancholic reflections on love, loss, and existence

## Common Themes

- **Dard** (दर्द): Physical and emotional pain
- **Gham** (ग़म): Grief and sorrow
- **Ranj** (रंज): Affliction and suffering
- **Udaasi** (उदासी): Melancholy and sadness
- **Zabt** (ज़ब्त): The struggle to contain one's pain

## The Philosophy of Dard

In Urdu poetry, pain is not merely negative - it is often seen as:
- A path to deeper understanding
- A measure of one's capacity to feel
- A connection to the divine or to greater truths
- An essential part of love and human experience
