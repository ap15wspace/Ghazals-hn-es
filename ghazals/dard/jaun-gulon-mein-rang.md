# गुलों में रंग भरे (Gulon Mein Rang Bhare)

**Poet:** Jaun Elia (जौन एलिया)  
**Theme:** Dard (Pain/Sorrow)  
**Era:** Contemporary (1931-2002)

---

## Original (Hindi/Urdu - Devanagari)

गुलों में रंग भरे बाद-ए-नौबहार चले  
चले भी आओ कि गुलशन का कारोबार चले

क़फ़स उदास है यारों सबा से कुछ तो कहो  
कहीं तो बहर-ए-ख़ुदा आज ज़िक्र-ए-यार चले

कभी तो सुब्ह तेरे कुंज-ए-लब से हो आगाज़  
कभी तो शब सर-ए-काकुल से मुश्कबार चले

बड़ा है दर्द का रिश्ता, ये दिल ग़रीब सही  
तुम्हारे नाम पे आएंगे ग़मगुसार चले

जो हम पे गुज़रती है रकीब, वो तुम पे कहें  
ग़रज़ गुज़ार हैं ग़म तुम पे क्यों गुज़ार चले

हमारे हिज्र में तुम को ग़म था या नहीं  
मगर ये रात तो यूं ही ख़ुमार के साथ गुज़ार चले

यही है आज़माइश तो सितम और सहो  
यही है ज़िंदगी तो यूं ही बेज़ार चले

हम ने माना कि कुछ नहीं 'जौन' मगर कुछ तो है  
तेरा ख़याल, दिल-ए-वीरान में संभाल चले

---

## Transliteration (Romanized)

Gulon mein rang bhare baad-e-naubahaar chale  
Chale bhi aao ki gulshan ka kaarobaar chale

Qafas udaas hai yaaron saba se kuch to kaho  
Kahin to bahr-e-Khuda aaj zikr-e-yaar chale

Kabhi to subh tere kunj-e-lab se ho aaghaaz  
Kabhi to shab sar-e-kaakul se mushkbaar chale

Bada hai dard ka rishta, ye dil ghareeb sahi  
Tumhare naam pe aayenge ghamgusaar chale

Jo hum pe guzarti hai raqeeb, wo tum pe kahen  
Gharaz guzaar hain gham tum pe kyun guzaar chale

Hamaare hijr mein tum ko gham tha ya nahin  
Magar ye raat to yun hi khumaar ke saath guzaar chale

Yahi hai aazmaaish to sitam aur saho  
Yahi hai zindagi to yun hi bezaar chale

Hum ne maana ki kuch nahin 'Jaun' magar kuch to hai  
Tera khayaal, dil-e-veeraan mein sambhaal chale

---

## Spanish Translation (Traducción Española)

Las flores se llenan de color, sopla la brisa de primavera  
Ven también para que el negocio del jardín prospere

La jaula está triste, amigos, díganle algo a la brisa  
Por el amor de Dios, que al menos hoy se mencione al amado

Que alguna vez el amanecer comience desde el rincón de tus labios  
Que alguna vez la noche pase perfumada desde tu cabellera

Grande es la conexión del dolor, aunque este corazón sea pobre  
En tu nombre vendrán quienes comparten el dolor

Lo que nos pasa, rival, díselo a ella  
El dolor está destinado a pasar, ¿por qué pasa sobre ti?

Si tuviste pena en nuestra separación o no  
Pero esta noche pasa así con embriaguez

Si esta es la prueba, entonces soporta más opresión  
Si esto es la vida, entonces sigue así, descontento

Acepto que no soy nada, 'Jaun', pero algo hay  
Tu recuerdo, lo conservo en este corazón desolado

---

## Context / Notes

This ghazal by Jaun Elia captures his characteristic blend of melancholy, existential angst, and deep romanticism. The opening line, which also appears as "Gulon mein rang bhare baad-e-naubahaar chale," is one of his most famous verses, celebrating the arrival of spring while simultaneously evoking a sense of longing.

Jaun Elia's poetry is known for its accessibility combined with philosophical depth. He wrote about love, pain, and existence in a way that resonated with modern audiences while maintaining classical ghazal traditions.

The ghazal explores themes of separation (hijr), the pain of unrequited love, and the philosophical acceptance of one's insignificance, which is a recurring theme in Elia's work. The final couplet is particularly poignant, acknowledging the poet's sense of worthlessness while asserting that the memory of the beloved gives meaning to his desolate heart.

---

## About the Poet

**Jaun Elia** (1931-2002) was a Pakistani poet, philosopher, and scholar who became a cult figure in Urdu literature. Known for his unconventional lifestyle and deeply introspective poetry, Elia wrote about existential themes, love, and pain with remarkable honesty. His only published collection during his lifetime, "Shayad" (Perhaps), appeared in 1991, though he had been writing for decades. His poetry gained immense popularity posthumously, especially among younger generations who connected with his themes of alienation and heartbreak.
