# Yaadein (Memories - Recuerdos)

This directory contains ghazals and shayari centered on the theme of **memories** (यादें - Yaadein).

## About This Theme

Nostalgia and reminiscence play an important role in Urdu poetry. These ghazals explore the bittersweet nature of memories, longing for the past, and the way moments from yesterday shape our today.

## Common Themes

- **Yaad** (याद): Memory and remembrance
- **Guzeeshta** (गुज़श्ता): The past
- **Kal** (कल): Yesterday/tomorrow
- **Nostalgia** (नोस्टैल्जिया): Nostalgia
- **Baatein** (बातें): Conversations and moments
- **Lamhe** (लम्हे): Moments in time

## What to Contribute

We welcome ghazals that explore:
- Cherished memories of the past
- Nostalgia for bygone days
- Lost moments with loved ones
- The passage of time
- How memories shape identity
