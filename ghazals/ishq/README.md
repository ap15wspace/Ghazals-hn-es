# Ishq (Love - Amor)

This directory contains ghazals and shayari centered on the theme of **love** (इश्क़ - Ishq).

## About This Theme

Love has been the central theme of Urdu/Hindi poetry for centuries. These ghazals explore various dimensions of love - romantic passion, devotion, the pain of separation from the beloved, and the transcendent nature of true love.

## Ghazals in This Collection

1. **Dil Hi To Hai** - Mirza Ghalib
   - Classic exploration of the heart's nature and its capacity for love and pain

2. **Mujh Se Pehli Si Mohabbat** - Faiz Ahmad Faiz
   - A profound reflection on how love competes with social consciousness

3. **Ranjish Hi Sahi** - Ahmad Faraz
   - The paradox of wanting to see the beloved even if it brings pain

4. **Aaj Jaane Ki Zid Na Karo** - Firaq Gorakhpuri
   - A tender plea for the beloved to stay

## Common Themes

- **Devotion** (इबादत - Ibadat): Love as worship
- **Longing** (तड़प - Tadap): Intense desire for the beloved
- **Union** (विसाल - Visaal): The joy of being with the beloved
- **Beauty** (हुस्न - Husn): Admiration of the beloved's beauty
- **Sacrifice** (क़ुर्बानी - Qurbani): What one gives up for love
